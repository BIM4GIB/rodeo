# Interiors and furniture

This page covers the Furniture/Fixtures tool set: tables, seating, wall fixtures, ceiling grids, railings, cabinets and countertops.

## Tables and seating

### Table and Chairs

With the **Table and Chairs** tool, click to place a rectangular table (key <kbd>1</kbd>) with a chair for each 600 mm along both long sides, and at its ends if Preferences say so, or a round one (key <kbd>2</kbd>) with the number of chairs set. A press dragged turns it.

### Seating Section

With the **Seating Section** tool, drag a rectangle (key <kbd>1</kbd>) or click a polygon (key <kbd>2</kbd>) as for [Space](architecture.md). Rows of chairs fill it, facing the way Preferences set, the first row at the front, seats centred along each row or every other row offset half a seat. A toast gives the seat count.

## Furniture and fixtures against a wall

The **Desk**, **Shelving Unit**, **Clothes Rod**, **Bath-Shower**, **Grab Bars**, **Fireplace**, **Toilet Stall**, **Cabinet** and **Compartment Sink** tools stand against a wall. A click puts the middle of the fixture's back on the point, and a press dragged turns it along the wall, its front to the drag's right. Sizes are in each tool's Preferences.

**Limits:** Vectorworks makes plug-in objects of these; these are plain objects, grouped, drawn as Rodeo draws them in plan.

### Desk

**Desk** is straight (key <kbd>1</kbd>) or has a left (key <kbd>2</kbd>) or right (key <kbd>3</kbd>) return, with a chair unless Preferences say not.

### Bath-Shower

**Bath-Shower** draws a bathtub (key <kbd>1</kbd>) with its drain at an offset from the basin's end, or a shower tray (key <kbd>2</kbd>) with falls to a middle drain.

### Toilet Stall

**Toilet Stall** draws a row of stalls, its partitions, doors (swinging in or out), toilets and turning circles as set in Preferences.

### Fireplace

**Fireplace** draws a unit narrowing to the wall with its firebox and a top flue.

### Cabinet

**Cabinet** draws one of:

- a base cabinet (key <kbd>1</kbd>) with its counter and back splash
- a utility cabinet (key <kbd>2</kbd>, crossed)
- a wall cabinet (key <kbd>3</kbd>, one diagonal)

### Compartment Sink

**Compartment Sink** draws a counter with its rolled edge and backsplash and a row of rounded bowls, each with a drain.

## Ceilings, railings, cabinets and countertops

### Ceiling Grid

With the **Ceiling Grid** tool, drag a rectangle (key <kbd>1</kbd>) or click a polygon (key <kbd>2</kbd>) as for [Space](architecture.md), for a ceiling with the lines between its tiles inside it. Their width, length and angle are in Preferences, with a tile centred in the ceiling or the grid laid from the drawing's origin.

### Railing/Fence

With the **Railing/Fence** tool, click each corner of its path, a double-click or <kbd>Enter</kbd> ending it, a click on the first corner closing it. It's drawn in plan with:

- its top rail
- posts (at the start, end and corners, and between them by approximate distance, fixed distance or number)
- frame bars or panels

### Custom Cabinet

With the **Custom Cabinet** tool, click to put the middle of its back on the point, a press dragged turning it. It draws its outline in plan, crossed with Show Diagonals.

### Countertop

The **Countertop** tool (Furniture/Fixtures tool set): click a countertop's corners, its backsplash a line inside the first edges and its other corners optionally rounded. Plain objects in plan.
