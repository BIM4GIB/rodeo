Questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The converter runs in your browser: your files are read and written on your computer and never sent anywhere.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no limits beyond your computer's memory. Rodeo is open source.

Can I convert to DWG?

Not yet: Rodeo doesn't write DWG. Convert to DXF, which AutoCAD and every DWG app open.

How big can a file be?

As big as your browser's memory allows. Very large Revit models do better with the command line, which has more room.

Can I convert many files, or script it?

Yes: npx @rodeobuild/cli convert "plans/*.dwg" --to pdf --out pdfs converts a folder at a time on your computer, and --json prints results for scripts.

On the command line: npx @rodeobuild/cli convert model.rvt model.ifc
